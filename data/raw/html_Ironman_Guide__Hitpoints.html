Ironman Guide

Hitpoints training is not significantly different on an ironman than a main account and is most often trained through combat. For more information about combat training on an Ironman, see:

  • Ironman Guide/Melee
  • Ironman Guide/Ranged
  • Ironman Guide/Magic

For general Hitpoints training advice, including non-combat methods, see pay-to-play Hitpoints training.
