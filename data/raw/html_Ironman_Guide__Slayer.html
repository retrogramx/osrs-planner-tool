Completing Slayer tasks is the sole way for Ironmen to acquire or upgrade several best-in-slot items, such as the abyssal whip, dragon boots, trident of the seas, occult necklace, and dragon hunter lance. High-level Slayer can also be an excellent way to earn money through valuable alchables.

Duradel is generally the best Slayer master to use. Konar quo Maten should be used for every milestone task for the extra Slayer reward points.

Using a dwarf multicannon is usually not worthwhile for efficient Slayer training on an Ironman due to the time required to make steel cannonballs, with the exception of smoke devils in some cases. Because of this, certain tasks which are decent on a main account, such as suqahs and kalphites, are not worth doing as an Ironman, as they have no notable drops and offer mediocre experience rates without a cannon. Blocking suqahs and kalphites should be one of your first priorities with Slayer points as an Ironman.

For tasks you want to finish quickly (usually when prioritising Slayer points), it is recommended to wear the expeditious bracelet, since it has a 25% chance to treat each kill as 2 kills towards the Slayer task. If you instead want to extend the task, a bracelet of slaughter will have a 25% chance for a kill to not count towards the Slayer task.

When using Slayer reward points, prioritise blocking and skipping poor tasks over unlocking perks and extensions. Unlock superior Slayer monsters, the Slayer helmet, and the ability to craft Slayer rings first. If you use Ranged often, consider unlocking Broader Fletching perk early on for the ability to fletch broad bolts.

Unless only focusing on quicker Magic levels, use defensive casting when bursting tasks (such as dust devils), since it gives more experience overall. If you are low on herb seeds, kill normal nechryael with melee instead of bursting greater nechryael in the Catacombs of Kourend.

Before you can kill abyssal demons for the abyssal whip, train only Strength after you have 75 Attack (to use Arclight) and 70 Defence (to use Barrows equipment), unless you want to hit combat level 100 sooner to access Duradel. Once you have obtained the abyssal whip, train on the Controlled style until you have 99 Strength, and after that train Attack and Defence. If you have a kraken tentacle, use any excess abyssal whips for an abyssal tentacle.

Starting off

[edit | edit source]

The natural history quiz has no requirements and rewards 1,000 Slayer experience, making for an easy way to reach level 9 Slayer.

To train Slayer efficiently, it is recommended that you begin after reaching 80–90 combat, with reasonable gear.

Recommended skills:

  • 70+ , and
  • 48 (Kourend Castle Teleport)
  • 43 (Protect from Melee)
  • 45 (super attack potions) or 55 (super strength potions)
  • 70 (Taverley Dungeon shortcut to blue dragons)
  • 27 for the ability to make emerald rings for rings of dueling

Using Ranged for low-level Slayer tasks in general is not recommended due to the poorer damage output of the available weapons and ammunition compared to dragon scimitar. However, some tasks can prove to be difficult or tedious to do without Prayer flicking or use of prayer potions, so safespotting with Ranged can be a justified option in some cases.

Point boosting

[edit | edit source]
Main article: Slayer reward points § Point boosting

Players can utilise Turael or Mazchna for point boosting. Mazchna is a viable alternative to Turael, as his tasks reward 6 points per task. Players might consider incorporating Mazchna into every 10th task while Turael skipping, using Konar quo Maten for every 50th task. See the main article above for more info.

Turael

[edit | edit source]

When starting Slayer training, the first priority is to block the most common bad tasks while reserving some Slayer points for skipping less common but equally bad tasks. It is therefore optimal to start by boosting Slayer points by using Turael and doing every 10th task at Konar quo Maten or the highest level Slayer master you are able to use. While Turael tasks do not give good experience, they are very easy and fast to complete unlike some of the tasks given by higher-level Slayer masters.

Turael can be quickly accessed via the games necklace's teleport to Burthorpe. Wearing heavy armour is not recommended, as you will be running a lot and the monsters you are killing do not deal much damage. Whenever you need to bank, teleport to the Ferox Enclave via a ring of dueling or use the minigame teleport and use the free-for-all portal to reset stats and run energy after banking.

Recommended inventory setup for Turael tasks:

  • Games necklace
  • Ring of dueling for resetting stats at the Ferox Enclave
  • 2 super attacks (and super strengths if not using a dragon battleaxe)
  • Runes for teleportation
  • Ardougne cloak 1 (or higher) and a dramen/lunar staff for access to fairy rings
  • Ectophial
  • Chronicle
  • A light source
  • Dragon battleaxe or dragon dagger for special attacks
  • Energy potions, antipoison (for cave crawlers and slimes) and some food (optional)

Additional information on Turael's tasks and where they can be done can be found at Slayer reward point#Turael boosting.

Once you have attained enough Slayer points, block these tasks as soon as you can:

  • Black demon
  • Hellhound
  • Suqah (Konar quo Maten does not assign these)
  • Kalphite (block last, Kalphite Workers are reasonably fast to kill)

Once you have these tasks blocked and some spare points for skipping, move on to the highest-level Slayer master you are able to use.

Krystilia (Wilderness Slayer)

[edit | edit source]

Alternatively, doing Wilderness Slayer tasks from Krystilia is another option for obtaining a good amount of Slayer points due to rewarding significantly more points per completed task than any other master. Krystilia has no requirements, but players should be sufficient enough to survive in the Wilderness and are strongly recommended to have at least 43 prayer for access to overhead prayers. When doing Wilderness tasks, only risk easily replaceable equipment you are willing to lose, such as monk robes or dragonhide armour. It's important to note that Krystilia can assign tasks of any combat level (Assuming skill and quest requirements are met), making this a potentially less preferable option at lower combat levels due to the chance of being given slow and/or difficult tasks early on.

Monsters killed for Wilderness tasks have the chance to drop Larran's keys, which can be used to open Larran's big chest for plenty of cash and useful resources like seeds, gems, ores, food and alchables. Slayer monsters within the Wilderness Slayer Cave have the added bonus of generally increased drop rates compared to their normal counterparts which, when using looting bags for an extra 28 inventory slots, make this a high risk but extremely high reward option of training Slayer. Lastly, monsters within the Wilderness can drop blighted food, blighted super restores and blighted rune sacks, all of which can only be used within the Wilderness and are particularly useful for ironmen as they add some level of self-sustainability and reduce the need to obtain these resources yourself.

Equipment

[edit | edit source]

Melee

[edit | edit source]

The aim for melee equipment is to maximise strength bonus.

Typical low/mid-level gear includes:

  • Slayer helmet (i): Can be assembled after purchasing the Malevolent Masquerade ability from a Slayer master, costing 400 Slayer reward points. Black mask can be obtained from cave horrors.
  • Any God blessing: Can be obtained from Treasure Trails. Alternatively, Rada's blessing 2 or higher can be used.
  • Fighter torso: Can be obtained from the Barbarian Assault minigame.
  • Blood moon chestplate/Blood moon tassets provide the same strength bonus as Bandos gear, but with higher magic defence that is good against magic-based tasks like Bloodvelds, Wyrms, and Aberrant Spectres.
  • Obsidian platelegs: Can be purchased in TzHaar-Hur-Zal's Equipment Store.
  • Climbing boots: Can be bought from Tenzing. Mixed hide boots are an alternative with higher defensive bonuses.
  • Barrows gloves: Can be purchased from Culinaromancer's Chest after completion of Recipe for Disaster.
  • Amulet of glory: Dragonstone amulet can be made at 80 Crafting and enchanted into an amulet of glory with Lvl-5 Enchant, or obtained from dragon implings.
  • Fire cape: Can be obtained as a reward for defeating TzTok-Jad in the Fight Caves.
  • Ring of wealth: Dragonstone ring can be made at 55 Crafting, which can be turned into a ring of wealth by Lvl-5 Enchant. Hardcore Ironmen should wear a ring of life on tasks which they can easily die if they disconnect.
  • Dragon scimitar: Can be purchased from Daga on Ape Atoll after completion of Monkey Madness I.
  • Dragon defender: Can be obtained as a rare drop in the basement of the Warriors' Guild from the cyclopes that reside there.
  • Zombie axe: Can be made with 70 smithing, after obtaining it in broken form as a rare drop from armoured zombies after starting Defender of Varrock.

Additional equipment

[edit | edit source]

The bonecrusher (requiring the hard Morytania diary), ash sanctifier (requiring the hard Kourend & Kebos diary), and ectoplasmator (purchasable from Soul Wars) can be worthwhile to bring on all tasks where you do not plan to pick up bones or ashes, as they will provide a passive source of Prayer experience. The bonecrusher specifically will also restore Prayer points when used within the Catacombs of Kourend, a popular location for many Slayer tasks. Bonecrusher charges can be easily sustained by the daily bonemeal reward from Robin.

Expeditious bracelets and bracelets of slaughter are recommended if desiring to finish an undesirable task quickly or extend a good task, respectively. They do not need to be worn the whole kill, only for the last hit, allowing stat-boosting gloves (such as Barrows gloves or a combat bracelet) to be worn for the rest of a kill.

Rings of wealth are a good choice for the ring slot if you have no stat-boosting rings (such as a berserker ring), as it automatically collects coin drops from monsters.

Turael skipping

[edit | edit source]

Higher levelled players may find that they wish to spend Slayer points en masse in an attempt to get one (or a few) specific tasks, such as cave kraken, smoke devil, hydra, greater demons, etc.

Players should avoid spending all of their points on skips to try and get specific tasks in this manner.

Instead, if players receive an undesired task from their Slayer master, they can return to Turael to sacrifice their Slayer streak and replace their task with a much faster one. Players can then complete Turael's task and return to their preferred Slayer master to try to get the task they want, rinse and repeat. Players that are about to begin Turael skipping should make sure that they are not too close to a major point streak, as they could be missing out on some free points in that case. Even regardless of that, the amount of points that players will save by not spending them on skips will greatly outweigh the loss of their streak. As far as for levelling Slayer, focusing on Turael skipping may not necessarily grant high Slayer experience rates, and may only be useful for getting specific tasks.

The inventory setup is similar to that of Point boosting with Turael, but higher level players can add things to the list such as: a rune pouch with NPC contact (if they have a POH with various portals), full graceful, a Royal seed pod, Salve amulet (e), and maybe even a Cannon and Cannonballs.

Killing bosses and demi-bosses on task

[edit | edit source]

Abyssal Sire (Abyssal demons)

[edit | edit source]

Abyssal Sire drops the Unsired, which can be placed at the Font of Consumption and gives the player a reward in the process. The most notable rewards are the abyssal bludgeon pieces and the abyssal dagger.

Alchemical Hydra (Hydras)

[edit | edit source]

The Alchemical Hydra notably drops the hydra's claw, which can be combined with a Zamorakian hasta to create a dragon hunter lance, one of the best items to have on an Ironman account. Additionally, the Alchemical Hydra also drops hydra leather, which can be made into ferocious gloves, and brimstone ring pieces. The Alchemical Hydra is also a generous source of money, Prayer experience, and herbs. Hydras can only be assigned by Konar quo Maten.

The twisted bow is the most effective weapon to kill the Alchemical Hydra, followed by the dragon hunter crossbow with ruby bolts (e) until it reaches 40% remaining Hitpoints, after which players should switch to using a toxic blowpipe. The toxic blowpipe is also viable throughout the whole kill should players not have either a twisted bow or dragon hunter crossbow, although its rapid consumption of Zulrah's scales should be noted, especially if planning to kill the boss enough to obtain all the unique drops.

Cerberus (Hellhounds)

[edit | edit source]

Cerberus drops the primordial, pegasian and eternal crystals, which are used to create best-in-slot boots in the game. Typically Cerberus is killed with an arclight, but you can still use a Zamorakian hasta/abyssal bludgeon/toxic blowpipe if you run out of charges. Hellhounds are very commonly assigned by Duradel.

Ironmen are strongly advised to use a different strategy which lets players often skip the Prayer-draining ghost phases entirely, letting players easily use less than a quarter of the Prayer potions that they would normally. The strategy revolves around keeping Cerberus above 400 health until 56 seconds passed from when she first attacked (or more precisely, after her second set of combo attacks, wait for 3 more regular attacks), before switching to your best DPS gear/spec weapons to finish her off. Players must be able to maintain more than ~7.5 DPS after the 56 seconds have passed to skip ghosts entirely. Using a full set of Guthan's equipment for the first 56 seconds is very important for extending trips with this strategy.

Dagannoth Kings (Dagannoths)

[edit | edit source]

Dagannoth Kings are killed for their unique drops (notably the rings and the dragon axe). After completion of the elite Fremennik Diary, killing the boss trio also offers good Prayer experience from the noted dagannoth bones.

Demonic gorillas (Black demons)

[edit | edit source]

Demonic gorillas drop zenyte shards, which can be used to make the best-in-slot jewellery. Demonic gorillas also drop good alchables and decent amount of high-level tree seeds.

Lizardman shamans (Lizardmen)

[edit | edit source]

Lizardman shamans drop the dragon warhammer, which has one of the most useful special attacks in the game. On task, the player can kill lizardman shamans in the Lizardman Caves which is often way less crowded than the Lizardman Canyon.

Kalphite Queen (Kalphites)

[edit | edit source]

Kalphite Queen is killed for Herblore and Farming supplies, most notable being the wines of Zamorak and potato cacti.

Kree'arra (Aviansie)

[edit | edit source]

Kree'arra is killed for Armadyl armour pieces.

K'ril Tsutsaroth (Greater demons)

[edit | edit source]

K'ril Tsutsaroth is mainly killed for the Zamorakian spear, which can be made into a Zamorakian hasta. Other notable uniques include the staff of the dead and Zamorak hilt. Typical setups use Arclight for demonbane damage and armour with high Magic defence. If you do not have Karil's leatherskirt, Void Knight robe gives slightly more Magic defence than black d'hide chaps. Players with a bow of Faerdhinen and crystal armour or a twisted bow will be able to get much faster kills using these weapons. Greater demons are commonly assigned by Duradel.

Sarachnis (Spiders)

[edit | edit source]

Sarachnis can be killed for Sarachnis cudgel, an early-game crush weapon, as well as a consistent supply of red spiders' eggs through the giant egg sac drops.

Tormented Demons (Greater demons)

[edit | edit source]

Tormented demons drop the tormented synapse, which is used to make the best-in-slot demonbane weaponry. This equipment is extremely strong for various demonic bosses like K'ril, Yama, and Skotizo. They also drop burning claws, which are a very strong special attack weapon for various bosses.

Vorkath (Blue dragons)

[edit | edit source]

Vorkath is killed for money, Prayer and Crafting experience.

Recommended tasks to do

[edit | edit source]

Dark beasts

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 11 (Duradel), 5 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Melee
  • Location: Mourner Tunnels
  • Extension: No
  • Bracelet: Expeditious
  • Experience per hour: ~30,000

Teleport to the Mourner Tunnels with a Slayer ring and run north to the dark beast area. Use Protect from Melee.

Greater Nechryael (magic)

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 9 (Duradel), 7 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Magic
  • Location: Catacombs of Kourend
  • Extension: Yes
  • Bracelet: Slaughter
  • Experience per hour: 75,000–95,000

Lure the monsters with darts, run around a corner until they stack up and kill with Ice Barrage. If low on health from the death spawns, cast Blood Barrage to heal up. Switch autocast to Blood Barrage when the superior spawns, or alternatively block the superior with other monsters so it cannot attack you. Bank all of the rune items.

Nechryael (melee)

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 9 (Duradel), 7 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Melee
  • Location: Slayer Tower
  • Extension: Yes
  • Bracelet: None
  • Experience per hour: ~31,000

Kill normal nechryael with melee in the Slayer Tower, use Arclight if you have charges. Bring runes for High Level Alchemy and a seed box if you have one. Saradomin godsword is very helpful for this task for its special attack, and allows the player to do the task in one trip.

When the superior spawns chaotic death spawns, run away until they become unaggressive. If you are doing the task upstairs, you can also climb down and up the chain to reset aggressiveness.

Smoke devils

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 9 (Duradel), 7 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Magic
  • Location: Smoke Devil Dungeon
  • Extension: N/A
  • Bracelet: Slaughter
  • Experience per hour: 75,000–80,000 (no cannon), 120,000–175,000 (cannon)

Lure Smoke Devils around the western bone pile and manually cast Ice Barrage on the eastern stack every 5 ticks. Run around bone pile to stack the monsters.

Making cannonballs for luring is only worthwhile if you are able to get good experience rates (160,000+ Slayer experience per hour). Otherwise it is faster overall to lure them with a shortbow, you can switch between rapid/longranged to lure more. You can now also use a Goading potion to maintain aggression.

Black dragons

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 9 (Duradel), 6 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Melee
  • Location: Taverley Dungeon
  • Extension: No
  • Bracelet: Expeditious
  • Experience per hour: ~30,000

Kill baby black dragons in the Taverley Dungeon. Banking the babydragon bones is recommended.

Dagannoths

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 9 (Duradel), 8 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Magic or Melee
  • Location: Catacombs of Kourend
  • Extension: N/A
  • Bracelet: Slaughter/expeditious (magic), none (melee)
  • Experience per hour: 55,000–80,000 (magic), 30,000 (melee)

Barraging dagannoths is a very difficult and expensive method, but fast experience per hour after some practice. Cast Ice Barrage to freeze a stack in the middle of the room, and run through them to get more monsters into the stack while manually casting Ice Barrage. Run around to aggro more while the stack is frozen. You will need 1–2 stamina potions per task if using this method.

If using Melee, simply turn on Protect from Melee and AFK.

Bring a seed box and soul bearer.

Metal dragons

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 14 (Duradel), 15 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Melee
  • Location: Various
  • Extension: No
  • Bracelet: Expeditious
  • Experience per hour: 25,000–30,000

All types of metal dragons can be killed for this task. This task should only be done using a dragon hunter lance or dragon hunter crossbow. Generally recommended to block this task.

Red dragons

[edit | edit source]
  • Weight: 8 (Duradel), 5 (Konar quo Maten)
  • Combat style: Melee
  • Location: Myths' Guild Dungeon
  • Extension: N/A
  • Bracelet: None
  • Experience per hour: ~30,000

This task should be unlocked only after getting a dragon hunter lance. Kill red dragons in the Myths' Guild Dungeon. Pick up the bones and the hides.

Players focusing on training Prayer may choose to unlock this task after unlocking the Sacred Bone Burner in the Forthos Dungeon.

Should Konar quo Maten assign you this task, only do it if it is located in the Myths' Guild Dungeon, or the Brimhaven Dungeon if you have Karamja elite diary completed.
